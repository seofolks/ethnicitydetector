About What Is My Ethnicity?

What Is My Ethnicity? is a free AI-powered tool that analyzes facial features to provide insights about ethnicity and emotions. Using advanced deep learning technology, our platform allows users to upload photos or use their webcam for instant analysis. We're committed to making cutting-edge facial analysis accessible to everyone while promoting ethical and responsible use. Our tool is designed for educational and entertainment purposes only—results are estimates based on AI analysis and should not be used for official identification or sensitive decisions.
