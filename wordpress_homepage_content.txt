Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How does the ethnicity detection work?
A: Our tool uses advanced AI models trained on diverse facial datasets to analyze facial features and provide ethnicity estimates. Results are based on facial morphology patterns and should be considered estimates, not definitive identifications.

Q: What emotions can the tool detect?
A: The tool can detect seven primary emotions: happy, sad, angry, surprised, fearful, disgusted, and neutral. Each emotion comes with a confidence score showing the likelihood.

Q: Is my photo stored or shared?
A: No. Your images are processed in real-time and immediately discarded. We don't store, share, or use your photos for any purpose beyond providing your analysis results.

Q: Can I use this for official purposes?
A: No. This tool is designed for informational and entertainment purposes only. It should not be used for official identification, legal matters, employment decisions, or any sensitive applications.

Q: What image formats are supported?
A: You can upload JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WebP images. For best results, use clear, high-quality photos with good lighting.

Q: Why are my results different from what I expected?
A: Ethnicity and emotion detection depend on many factors including image quality, lighting, facial angle, and the inherent limitations of AI models. Results are estimates based on facial features and should be interpreted with this understanding.

Q: Is the tool free to use?
A: Yes! Our ethnicity and emotion detection tool is completely free to use with no sign-up required.
